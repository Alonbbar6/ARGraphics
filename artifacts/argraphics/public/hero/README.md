# Hero plate

`plate.jpg` is the photo behind the hero's 3D special. Replace it with your own venue
photo (landscape, ~2000px wide, dark and moody works best; the drink is composited over
the right half).

Current placeholder: "A dimly lit bar with stools and bottles on the shelves" by
Shawn DENG on Unsplash (https://unsplash.com/photos/nsgzQHsxbXE), Unsplash License.

`../og.jpg` is the 1200×630 social preview (Open Graph / Twitter card), a capture of the
hero mid-scroll. Regenerate it after changing the hero photo or the drink.
